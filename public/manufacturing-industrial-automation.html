Common industrial robot configurations

Articulated arms

Flexible multi-axis systems for welding, assembly, machine tending, and handling.

Collaborative robots (cobots)

Systems designed for collaborative applications, subject to appropriate risk assessment and safeguards.

SCARA

Fast, precise movement for repetitive assembly and pick-and-place operations.

Cartesian / Gantry

Linear-motion robots suited to machine loading and handling across defined workspaces.

Delta

High-speed picking, sorting, and packaging of lightweight products.

Specialized configurations

Cylindrical, polar, and other purpose-built systems for specific industrial tasks.
