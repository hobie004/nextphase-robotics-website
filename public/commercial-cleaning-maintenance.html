Practical selection criteria

What we evaluate before recommending equipment

Coverage per shift

Measure usable cleaning coverage after allowing for charging, refilling and staff intervention.

Floor conditions and obstacles

Check thresholds, slopes, floor finishes, changing obstacles and operation around people.

Consumables and upkeep

Include brushes, pads, cleaning solution, batteries and routine maintenance in the ownership cost.

Demonstrated labor-value savings

Compare supervised operating time with the manual process; count staff time needed to prepare and recover the machine.
