Field conditions and reliability

Match traction, terrain, dust and weather tolerance to the actual field conditions and working season.

Operating economics and seasonality

Compare useful operating hours, seasonal utilization and downtime with the current equipment and crew costs.

Training and local support

Confirm who trains operators, diagnoses faults and supplies replacement parts during peak season.

Ownership and deployment model

Establish whether equipment is purchased, leased or service-operated and what the customer can maintain.
