Build the business case

Measure the work. Then evaluate the technology.

Establish a baseline

Document labor hours, output, quality, rework and the difficult or repetitive parts of the current process.

Include the whole cost

Consider equipment, integration, training, consumables, energy, maintenance and the supervision still required.

Validate in the application

Use demonstrations, references or a scoped pilot to evaluate performance before committing to a wider rollout.

Labor capacity recovered is not automatically a cash saving. A useful business case distinguishes avoided overtime, work reassigned to the team, additional throughput and actual cost reductions. Results depend on the equipment and operating conditions.
