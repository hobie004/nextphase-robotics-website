Practical selection criteria

What we evaluate before recommending equipment

Turf quality and cutting height

Match cut quality, height, slopes and turning behavior to the specific turf and maintenance standard.

Real-world coverage and labor value

Compare effective acres or balls recovered per shift, charging time and staff supervision with the current operation.

Safety around golfers and staff

Plan separation from golfers, staff and vehicles, including operating windows and manufacturer safety controls.

Local parts, warranty and technical support

Confirm parts availability, response times, warranty responsibilities and local technical support before purchase.
